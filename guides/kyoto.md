# Kyoto

## Visa and entry
Kyoto follows Japan's entry rules: US citizens can visit visa-free for tourism for up to 90 days with a passport valid for the whole stay. Kyoto has no airport; most international travelers fly into Osaka Kansai (KIX).

## Getting around
From Kansai Airport, the JR Haruka express reaches Kyoto Station in about 75 minutes. City buses are cheap but crowded; the two subway lines are faster for some routes. Walking and renting a bicycle work well in the flat central area. Nara is an easy day trip by train.

## Etiquette
In Gion, do not follow or photograph geiko and maiko without permission, and respect signs banning photos in private alleys, where fines apply. Temples and gardens expect quiet visitors. Tipping is not practiced.

## Money
Many temples and shrines charge entrance fees of roughly 400 to 1,000 yen and often accept cash only. Carry cash for small restaurants and markets.

## Neighborhoods and where to stay
Higashiyama has preserved streets leading to Kiyomizu-dera. Gion is the historic entertainment district. Arashiyama has the bamboo grove. Fushimi is home to Fushimi Inari Taisha and its thousands of torii gates.
