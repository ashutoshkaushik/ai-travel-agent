# London

## Visa and entry
US citizens do not need a visa for tourist visits, but they need an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) approved before travel. Apply through the official UK ETA app or GOV.UK website, not third-party sites.

## Getting around
Tap a contactless card or phone on the Tube and buses; daily fares are capped automatically. From Heathrow (LHR), choose the Heathrow Express (fastest), the Elizabeth line, or the Piccadilly line (cheapest).

## Etiquette
Stand on the right and walk on the left on escalators. Queue patiently. Many restaurants add a 10 to 12.5 percent service charge; check the bill before tipping extra.

## Money
Many shops and cafés are card-only, so cash is rarely needed. Major museums such as the British Museum and the National Gallery are free to enter.

## Neighborhoods and where to stay
Westminster has Parliament and Big Ben. The South Bank has theaters and river walks. Covent Garden is busy with shops and street performers. Shoreditch is known for street art and food. Kensington has the major museums.
