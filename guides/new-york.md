# New York

## Visa and entry
For US citizens, New York is a domestic trip: no passport or visa is needed. To pass airport security on domestic flights you need a REAL ID-compliant driver's license or another accepted ID such as a passport.

## Getting around
The subway runs 24 hours a day; tap a contactless card or phone at OMNY readers. From JFK, the AirTrain connects to the subway and the Long Island Rail Road. Yellow cabs and ride-hailing are available everywhere but slow in traffic.

## Etiquette
Tipping is expected: about 18 to 20 percent at restaurants and bars. Walk briskly and step aside before stopping on busy sidewalks.

## Money
Sales tax is added at the register, and hotel stays carry additional city and state taxes, so the final bill is higher than the listed rate. Many museums have pay-what-you-wish hours; check each museum's site.

## Neighborhoods and where to stay
Midtown has Times Square and the Empire State Building. Lower Manhattan has the 9/11 Memorial and Wall Street. Greenwich Village is known for cafés and jazz clubs. In Brooklyn, DUMBO has skyline views and Williamsburg has food and nightlife.
