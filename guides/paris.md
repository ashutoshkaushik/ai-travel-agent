# Paris

## Visa and entry
US citizens can visit France and the Schengen area visa-free for up to 90 days in any 180-day period. Your passport should be valid for at least 3 months beyond your planned departure. The EU Entry/Exit System registers a photo and fingerprints at the border, and the ETIAS travel authorization is planned for visa-free visitors; check official EU sources for its current status before you travel.

## Getting around
The Metro is the fastest way around; pay with a Navigo Easy card or contactless where accepted. The RER B train connects Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG) to central Paris in about 35 minutes. Central Paris is very walkable.

## Etiquette
Say "Bonjour" when entering a shop or café and "Au revoir" when leaving. Restaurant prices include service, so tipping is optional; rounding up or leaving small change is common.

## Money
Cards are accepted almost everywhere. Many museums close one day a week: the Louvre closes on Tuesdays. Book popular museums and the Eiffel Tower summit in advance.

## Neighborhoods and where to stay
Le Marais has historic streets and boutiques. The Latin Quarter is lively and student-filled. Saint-Germain-des-Prés has classic cafés. Montmartre has Sacré-Cœur and views over the city.
