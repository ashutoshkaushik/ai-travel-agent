# Singapore

## Visa and entry
US citizens can visit Singapore visa-free for up to 90 days. Submit the SG Arrival Card online within 3 days before you arrive; use the official Immigration and Checkpoints Authority website or app.

## Getting around
The MRT is clean, fast, and air-conditioned; tap a contactless card or phone. Changi Airport (SIN) connects to the city by MRT in about 30 minutes, and taxis are reasonably priced.

## Etiquette
Singapore enforces its rules strictly: littering brings fines, chewing gum is not sold, and eating or drinking on the MRT is prohibited. Tipping is not customary because bills usually include a 10 percent service charge plus GST.

## Money
Hawker centres serve excellent meals for a few dollars. Prices in restaurants and shops generally include or add GST. Cards and mobile payments are accepted almost everywhere.

## Neighborhoods and where to stay
Marina Bay has Gardens by the Bay and the skyline. Chinatown has temples and food streets. Little India is colorful and lively. Kampong Glam centers on the Sultan Mosque. Sentosa is the resort island.
